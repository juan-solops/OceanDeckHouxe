Discover Abades & South Tenerife

From world-class diving to hidden beaches and authentic Canarian culture, Abades offers the perfect blend of adventure and tranquility.

World-Class Diving

Crystal-clear waters, volcanic reefs, and diverse marine life make Abades one of South Tenerife's premier diving destinations.

Hidden Beaches

Discover secret coves and natural volcanic beaches away from the tourist crowds along South Tenerife's stunning coastline.

Hiking Trails

Explore dramatic coastal paths and mountain trails with breathtaking views of the Atlantic and Mount Teide.

Local Cuisine

Authentic Canarian restaurants serving fresh seafood and traditional dishes in charming village settings.

Teide National Park

Spain's highest peak and a UNESCO World Heritage site is just 90 minutes from Abades.

Remote Work Paradise

Fast fiber WiFi, peaceful environment, and year-round sunshine make Abades perfect for digital nomads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Ocean Deck House in Abades, Arico, Tenerife.

Is there parking available at Ocean Deck House?

Yes, there is free parking available near the property. Abades is a small, quiet village with easy access by car from the TF-1 motorway.

How far is Ocean Deck House from Tenerife South Airport?

Tenerife South Airport (TFS) is approximately 25–30 minutes by car. Car hire is recommended to explore the area freely.

How fast is the WiFi at Ocean Deck House?

We have fibre optic broadband installed, offering fast and reliable internet — perfect for remote workers and streaming.

How many guests can stay at Ocean Deck House?

Ocean Deck House accommodates up to 4 guests comfortably.

What is there to do near Ocean Deck House in Abades?

Abades is renowned for world-class diving and snorkelling. Nearby activities include hiking, beach visits, water sports at El Médano, and day trips to Teide National Park.
