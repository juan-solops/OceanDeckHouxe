Discover Abades & Arico

Abades is one of Tenerife's best-kept secrets — a tranquil coastal village in the municipality of Arico, on the south-east coast. Far from the tourist crowds, it offers crystal-clear waters, dramatic volcanic landscapes, and an authentic Canarian atmosphere.


The area is especially beloved by divers, snorkellers, nature lovers, and anyone seeking a peaceful, genuine Tenerife experience.

Things To Do

What To Do in Abades & Arico

Diving & Snorkelling

Abades is world-renowned for its exceptional diving. The crystal-clear, calm waters and rich marine life make it one of the top dive spots in Tenerife.

  • Abades natural marine reserve
  • Underwater volcanic rock formations
  • Abundant sea life: rays, octopus, barracuda
  • Suitable for all levels, from beginners to experts

Hiking & Nature

The Arico municipality offers stunning volcanic landscapes and hiking trails with breathtaking views of the Atlantic Ocean and Mount Teide.

  • Coastal walks along the cliffs of Arico
  • Views of Mount Teide from the south
  • Volcanic rock pools and natural formations
  • Birdwatching in the rural interior

Beaches & Coves

Discover hidden coves and natural beaches near Abades, away from the crowded tourist spots of the south.

  • Playa de Abades – calm, clear waters
  • Nearby Playa de la Jaquita
  • El Médano beach (20 min) – windsurfing paradise
  • Playa de Los Abrigos (15 min)

Local Food & Restaurants

Experience authentic Canarian cuisine in local restaurants and fish taverns near Abades and along the coast.

  • Fresh fish and seafood at local guachinches
  • Traditional papas arrugadas with mojo sauce
  • Los Abrigos fishing village restaurants (15 min)
  • Local markets in Arico town

Water Sports

The south-east coast of Tenerife is a hotspot for water sports, thanks to consistent winds and calm waters.

  • Windsurfing & kitesurfing at El Médano
  • Kayaking along the coastline
  • Stand-up paddleboarding (SUP)
  • Boat trips and whale watching nearby

Culture & Day Trips

Explore the rich culture and stunning landscapes of Tenerife with easy day trips from Abades.

  • Teide National Park (1 hr) – UNESCO World Heritage
  • Santa Cruz de Tenerife city (40 min)
  • Siam Park water park (30 min)
  • Arico old town & traditional architecture
